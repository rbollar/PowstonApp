# Glossary

**Powston**: the service at powston.com: the controller that runs a site's battery decisions, its web app, its REST API and its MCP server. Not our app.

**The app**: our Powston client for iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple TV (bundle prefix `com.bollar.Powston`).

**Private Cloud Compute (PCC)**: Apple's server-side Apple Intelligence model, reached through the Foundation Models framework. Contrast with the **on-device model**, which runs offline on the phone.

**Tool**: a question the model may ask the app during an answer (for example, the battery's current charge). Our tools only read; they never change a site.
